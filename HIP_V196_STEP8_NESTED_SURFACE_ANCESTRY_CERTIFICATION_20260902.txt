HIP Portal Agent v1.9.6 — Step 8 Certification
Date: 2026-09-02

Scope
-----
This release hardens nested detached-surface continuity after the v1.9.5
continuous surface lease. It carries proven structural provenance across
menu -> dialog -> drawer/listbox/confirmation transitions and prevents
continuation actions from escaping to same-named global page controls.

Implemented behavior
--------------------
- AutoWebGLM remains the primary browser-decision policy layer.
- Existing semantic affordance, repeatable-row identity, compound-menu,
  detached-overlay provenance, virtualized option scrolling and v1.9.5
  surface-continuity lease remain enabled.
- BrowserSession maintains a bounded structural surface ancestry chain.
- Maximum nested surface chain depth: 8.
- Continuation intents prefer the deepest still-proven child surface:
  Edit, Clone, Migrate, Deploy, Delete, Save, Create, Next, Back, Close,
  Retry, Upload and Download.
- If a valid active child does not contain the requested continuation action,
  execution fails closed; no global same-named fallback is allowed.
- A newly opened child inherits provenance only from the exact structural
  surface delta caused by the proven opener/action.
- When a child closes, it is pruned and the nearest still-visible parent is
  re-proven before further execution.
- The ancestry chain is cleared on HIP module navigation.
- Surface ancestry evidence is structural only; customer-entered values are
  not stored in the chain.
- Mutation actions remain subject to the existing mutation-governance gate.
