Layer

Continuous detached-surface provenance lease + stable target membership guard.

Problem fixed
-------------
v1.9.4 proved which detached Angular/CDK/DDS overlay belonged to a scoped opener, but the proof was effectively selector-based after initial binding. A live portal can destroy/recreate that overlay during asynchronous loading, filtering, virtual scrolling, or rerender, and a stale duplicate surface can remain visible. A selector that was correct at T0 is therefore not durable surface identity at T1.

Implemented behavior
--------------------
- Detached-overlay provenance is now a continuously revalidated lease, not a one-time selector decision.
- Before every semantic target read and every virtualized scroll, the runtime snapshots visible actionable surfaces and re-proves the leased surface.
- Rebinding prefers opener aria-controls / aria-owns identity, then exact surface ID continuity, then one unique structural continuation.
- Structural continuity uses surface kind/role, accessible label/title, bounded action overlap, and geometry.
- Selector equality is only weak evidence and cannot independently transfer trust to a replacement surface.
- Two similarly plausible stale/current replacement surfaces fail closed.
- Surface-scoped targets require consecutive stable semantic reads before the normal compound-action executor accepts them.
- Immediately before click, the runtime re-proves the surface again and requires exactly one visible matching target contained by exactly one proven surface.
- A same-named global Edit/Deploy/etc. therefore cannot inherit a detached-menu action selector.
- No global fallback is allowed once detached-surface provenance has been established.
- Existing AutoWebGLM primary planning, AgentQ gates, mutation governance, Edge session ownership, effect verification, repeatable-row identity, compound actions, detached overlay binding, and virtualized scrolling remain intact.
